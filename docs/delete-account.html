Status Maker by AppBeDev

Delete your account and data

Applies to the Status Maker app for Android (package com.appbedev.statusmaker)

In the app (instant)

  • Open Status Maker.
  • Tap the Settings tab (gear icon, bottom right).
  • Tap Delete my data, then confirm.

Your data is deleted immediately and the app continues as a brand-new anonymous user.

By email (if you can't open the app)

Email appbedev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Accounts are anonymous, so include something that lets us find your statuses, such as their titles, categories or screenshots. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous account.
  • Every status you saved or posted, including public posts in the feed.
  • The photos and preview images stored for them.
  • Your likes, notifications, reports you sent, and your device's notification token.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Our hosting provider's server request logs (IP address and request time) are kept for a limited period for security, then deleted automatically.
  • Customised copies other users made of your public posts belong to them. Their text and layout stay; images stored in your folder are removed.
  • Images you already shared to other apps (such as WhatsApp) are outside our control.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. See our Privacy Policy for details.
