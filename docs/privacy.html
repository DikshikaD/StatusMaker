Who we are

Status Maker is made by AppBeDev ("we", "us"). It lets you design picture statuses from templates, save them, post them to a shared feed, and share them to apps such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and the app has no sign-up screen.

Photos you pick but don't save stay on your device. We don't read your photo library beyond the photo you choose.

How we use it

  • To keep your saved statuses so you can reopen and edit them later.
  • To show posted statuses in the public feed, with their like counts.
  • To let other users customise a posted status as a starting point for their own.
  • To send push notifications when someone likes or customises your status, worded in your chosen language.
  • To review reports and remove content that breaks our Terms.
  • To keep the service working and secure, for example to stop misuse.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't build profiles of you, and we don't sell it.

What other people can see

  • Posted statuses are public. Anyone using the app can see them, like them, share the image and customise a copy. Photos inside a posted status are visible as part of it.
  • Saved statuses that you haven't posted are visible only to you in the app. Their preview image and photos are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses, so treat anything you upload as something that could be seen by a person who obtains its address.
  • Other users never see your account ID in the app. Like counts are shown, but not who liked. Reports you send are not shown to the creator.

Don't put information in a posted status that you wouldn't want the public to see.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase (privacy policy) hosts our database, file storage and anonymous accounts.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (privacy information) delivers push notifications using your device's notification token.
  • Google Fonts (privacy information) supplies the typefaces used in the app. The app downloads them from Google's servers, so Google receives your device's IP address and the font requested. No account ID or content is sent.

When you tap Share, the image goes to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). What happens there is covered by that app's own policy.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Device permissions

  • Photos / camera: only when you choose to add a photo to a status.
  • Notifications: to tell you about likes and customisations. You can turn this off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Internet: to load the feed and save your statuses.

How long we keep it

  • Statuses, photos and likes are kept until you delete them or delete your data.
  • Notification tokens are replaced when they change and removed when they stop working.
  • Read notifications are removed automatically after 30 days.
  • Reports are kept while we review them and for as long as needed to keep the service safe.

Deleting your data

One status: open it in My Creations or the feed and tap Delete.

Everything at once: in the app, open Settings and tap Delete my data. This permanently removes your statuses (including posted ones), the photos and previews stored for them, your likes, notifications, device tokens and your anonymous account. It takes effect immediately and can't be undone.

Can't open the app? Email appbedev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include something that lets us find your statuses, such as their titles. We'll delete everything within 30 days and confirm by email.

Uninstalling the app removes the preferences stored on your phone, but not what's on our servers, and the anonymous account can't be recovered afterwards. Delete your data first if you want it gone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can delete your data yourself in the app; for anything else, email us at the address below. We'll reply within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each account read only public statuses and its own private ones, and change only its own. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Status Maker isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has posted content, report it in the app or email us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes we'll also let you know in the app.
