Status Maker helps you create beautiful picture statuses in Hindi and English for every morning, festival, birthday and moment worth sharing.

Pick a ready-made template, add your photo and name, choose a quote, and share it with family and friends in one tap.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Ready-made templates for Good Morning (सुप्रभात), Good Night, festivals, birthdays, love, motivation, devotional and suvichar
• 200+ Hindi and English quotes, or write your own
• 100+ designer backgrounds, plus gradients, stickers, shapes and stylish fonts
• Add your own photo, then move, zoom and frame it
• Edit everything: text, colours, size, position and frames
• Save your designs privately and edit them again any time
• Post to the community feed, and like or customise other people's designs
• Share your finished status to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or any other app
• Get notified when someone likes or customises your post
• App available in Hindi and English, with light and dark themes

START INSTANTLY
No sign-up, no login, no email or phone number needed.

A SAFE COMMUNITY
Report, hide or block any post or creator. Posts that break our community rules are removed.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
No ads and no tracking. Delete everything you created at any time from Settings → Delete my data.

Status Maker is an independent app and is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or Meta.
